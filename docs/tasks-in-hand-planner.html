How a task moves on the planner

A task appears only on the day it is planned for. Overdue work is not repeated on later days: it stays red on the day it was missed, and the row shows how many tasks are overdue now (click the number for the list).

Where a task sitsOn its deadline day, or on the day the assignee added it to their day plan.
Past days keep their logIf the day had already arrived when the task moved, that day keeps a struck-through entry saying where it went and who moved it.
Future days are clearedIf the task moves before the day arrives (re-planned, pulled forward by the PM, or taken into today's plan), it simply disappears from that future day, week and month.
Missed countEach time a deadline passes undone and is then moved, the count goes up: 1st, 2nd, 3rd deadline missed. The badge travels with the task to its new day.

What this mockup covers

  1. Task titles in Day view. Each cell lists the tasks as Project → Title (Assignee → Title on the Project tab) instead of a count.
  2. Earlier periods. Day shows the full week Mon to Sun, including past days. The arrows move a week, six weeks or six months back or forward; Today jumps back.
  3. Colour per task and per cell. Green done, blue planned, teal taken into a day plan, red not done that day. A past or current cell turns green when everything planned that day is done, yellow when part is done, red when nothing is.
  4. Moves and missed deadlines. Deadline moved later after it passed: "Nth deadline missed" on the new day, struck-through log on the old one. Taken into today's plan or pulled forward by the PM: shown today and removed from the future day, week and month.
  5. Planned vs done bars. Every column header shows done out of planned for everyone in the filter; each row shows the same for that person or project. Week and Month count each task once per period.
  6. Filters. Assignee tab gets a multi-select Assignee filter; Project tab gets a multi-select Project filter. Everything mirrors between the two tabs.
