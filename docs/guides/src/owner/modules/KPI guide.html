KPI

Management overview

The management overview of every project and department. Period: Day, Week or Month; "closed" and "on-time" compare with the period before.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
Day, Week or MonthThe period changes only the glowing data points: Closed, On-time, Project health (closed in the period) and People (delivered in the period), each compared with the period before. Open, On hold, Overdue, Active projects and No deadline are counted as of now, whatever the period. Every task in every project, all departments. A task is closed when it first reaches QA Backlog (or, without QA stages, when Bitrix24 closes it).
Open tasksPending tasks: behind QA Backlog and not on hold. Department = the assignee’s department. Click any count to list those tasks below.
OverdueOpen tasks whose deadline has passed. "Over 30 days" = more than 30 days late.
ClosedTasks closed in the period (first QA Backlog, else the Bitrix24 closed date). Clean-ups of 30+ tasks closed in one project on one day are left out.
On-timeOf the tasks closed in the period that had a deadline, the share closed on or before it.
Active projectsProjects with open tasks and any action (task change or comment) in the last 30 days. Silent = open tasks and no action for 10+ working days.
No deadlineOpen tasks without a deadline: they cannot be overdue or planned.
InsightsFindings worked out from the same numbers. Click one to see its tasks. Start here each morning. Click the title to focus it in the centre; drag ⠿ at the top to move it.
Project healthOne row per project, worst first. Red: over 25 % of open tasks overdue (at least 3), or a task over 30 days late. Amber: any overdue task, or silent. Green: neither.
Overdue breakdownOpen tasks past their deadline, by how many days late, per project and per person.
WorkloadOpen project tasks per person: overdue, due in the next 10 working days, due later, no deadline.
PeoplePer person: tasks closed in the period (vs the one before), on-time %, open and overdue now, QA send-backs on closed tasks, and closed tasks over the last 8 periods.
ActivityBusiest projects of the last 7 days: new tasks, bugs raised, stage moves and comments (ranked by new×2 + bugs×3 + moves + comments). Silent projects below.
All tasksEvery number on this tab opens its tasks here. Edit in place, bulk edit and Excel export work as on the other tabs.
