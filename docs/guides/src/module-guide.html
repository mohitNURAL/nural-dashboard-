01

On every page

The parts every page shares

PartWhat it does
The menuEvery page is here: the dashboard tabs and, for managers, the Employee module. Hover the icons to open it; click ☰ to pin it open, and again to unpin.
FiltersProject, assignee, tag and date range narrow every number on the tab. Reset filters clears them. The "i" icons say exactly what each number counts.
AlertsThe bell counts overdue tasks and tasks stuck over 5 days in one stage. Click it for the list and jump to the tasks.
LiveLive means changes in Bitrix24 reach the dashboard within seconds; numbers refresh by themselves. Offline means the connection dropped and it is reconnecting.
Page toolsOn the right: Customise (choose the tiles and cards to show), the guided tour of this page, the PDF guides, reading tours aloud, and resetting the layout. Hover any icon for its name. Each data point also has a small toolbar on hover: ⠿ to move it, ? to explain it, the eye to hide it.
Your profileYour name and role, the theme (Light, Dark, System), live-update toasts, Reset layout, this tour (replay, voice on or off) and Sign out. You are signed out 3 days after signing in.
02

KPI

Management overview

AdminsPMTeam leadsProduct owners

The management overview of every project and department. Period: Day, Week or Month; "closed" and "on-time" compare with the period before.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
Day, Week or MonthThe period for closed, on-time and the people table, compared with the period before. Every task in every project, all departments. A task is closed when it first reaches QA Backlog (or, without QA stages, when Bitrix24 closes it).
Open tasksPending tasks: behind QA Backlog and not on hold. Department = the assignee’s department. Click any count to list those tasks below.
OverdueOpen tasks whose deadline has passed. "Over 30 days" = more than 30 days late.
ClosedTasks closed in the period (first QA Backlog, else the Bitrix24 closed date). Clean-ups of 30+ tasks closed in one project on one day are left out.
On-timeOf the tasks closed in the period that had a deadline, the share closed on or before it.
Active projectsProjects with open tasks and any action (task change or comment) in the last 30 days. Silent = open tasks and no action for 10+ working days.
No deadlineOpen tasks without a deadline: they cannot be overdue or planned.
InsightsFindings worked out from the same numbers. Click one to see its tasks. Start here each morning. Click the title to focus it in the centre; drag ⠿ at the top to move it.
Project healthOne row per project, worst first. Red: over 25 % of open tasks overdue (at least 3), or a task over 30 days late. Amber: any overdue task, or silent. Green: neither.
Overdue breakdownOpen tasks past their deadline, by how many days late, per project and per person.
WorkloadOpen project tasks per person: overdue, due in the next 10 working days, due later, no deadline.
PeoplePer person: tasks closed in the period (vs the one before), on-time %, open and overdue now, QA send-backs on closed tasks, and closed tasks over the last 8 periods.
ActivityBusiest projects of the last 7 days: new tasks, bugs raised, stage moves and comments (ranked by new×2 + bugs×3 + moves + comments). Silent projects below.
All tasksEvery number on this tab opens its tasks here. Edit in place, bulk edit and Excel export work as on the other tabs.
03

Development

Tasks, issues and deadlines

EveryoneMembers: own tasks

The Development department's tasks and issues. The All / Dev / Issues switch at the top steers every number.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
All, Dev or IssuesDevelopment department only. Dev = development tasks, Issues = bugs and feedback, All = both. Every number on this tab follows this switch.
TotalAll tasks in the filters for the switch (Dev, Issues or both), Development department only. Under the number: the split into Dev and Issues (or Bug and Feedback). Click any count to list those tasks below.
For members: Your tasks (assigned to you or created by you) for the switch. Click any count to list those tasks below.
PendingBehind QA Backlog and not on hold: To do, In progress, Pushed to Master, Merged to Development, Pending for QA, Issue Raised. Click any count to list those tasks below.
On holdIn On Hold: neither pending nor completed. Pending + completed + on hold = total.
Ready to deployIn the Done stage, ready to go live. Then Deployed: in the LIVE stage.
OverduePending, not On Hold, and the deadline has passed. The first number to act on each day.
Done todayReached QA Backlog for the first time today (IST).
Per person or projectPer person or project. Done = sent to QA (QA Backlog to LIVE). Comp. today = reached QA Backlog today. Switch Assignee / Project at the top. Every count opens its tasks. Click the title to focus it in the centre; drag ⠿ at the top to move it.
Pending vs DonePending vs completed (QA Backlog, In QA, Done, LIVE) per person or project, top 15 by total. Tooltip splits Dev / Issues (Bug / Feedback on Issues). Click a bar to open those tasks.
Task ageingOpen tasks by days past deadline. On Hold counts here but not as Overdue. The darker the red, the later. Chart or Table at the top.
On-time rankingTasks sent to QA with a deadline. Ranked by on-time %, then average days early. Fewer than 3: not ranked.
Tasks by stageTasks per Kanban stage; the colour bar shows each stage total. The red part of a cell is overdue. Click a cell for those tasks.
Task listsDue today, Overdue, All tasks and Alert details open here. Clicking any number above opens All tasks narrowed to it. In a list: click a cell to change stage, status or tags (and more for leads), tick rows for bulk edit, and export to Excel.
For members: Due today, Overdue, All tasks and Alert details open here. Clicking any number above opens All tasks narrowed to it. In a list you can change stage, status and tags of your tasks, tick rows for bulk edit, and export to Excel.

Task lists

Due today, Overdue, All tasks (follows the last number you clicked) and Alert details. Each list sorts, edits in place, bulk edits, pages and exports to Excel.

04

QA

Bugs, feedback, testing and retesting

EveryoneMembers: own tasks

Bugs and feedback (tasks tagged bug or feedback), testing work and the retest queue. The Bug or Feedback filter narrows the page.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
Issues filedEvery task tagged bug or feedback in the filters, whoever it is assigned to. Bug and Feedback under the number. Click any count to list those tasks below.
For members: Bugs and feedback assigned to you or filed by you. Click any count to list those tasks below.
Open / pendingOpen = pending: any stage behind QA Backlog except On Hold (Issue Raised included). Resolved = QA Backlog, In QA, Done or LIVE. On Hold is its own tile. The ring shows the resolved %.
On holdIn On Hold: neither pending nor completed. Pending + completed + on hold = total.
Last filed and resolvedLatest day an issue was created, and how many that day. Next to it, Last resolved: Latest day issues were pushed to QA Backlog (a fix handed to QA), and how many that day.
ReworkIssues QA sent back after testing: from QA Backlog, In QA or Done to Issue Raised, To do or In progress. Stage flow only.
Retesting awaitedEvery task waiting for QA: in QA Backlog or In QA, split into dev tasks and issues (bug or feedback). The QA team's queue.
Issues by personOpen vs resolved, each split into bug and feedback (or the reverse), per person or project. Table: the assignee-wise table. Click the title to focus it in the centre; drag ⠿ at the top to move it.
Rework by developerQA send-backs per person or project, split into bug and feedback, and the % of their issues sent back. First Issue Raised to To do is not counted.
Testing tasksTasks tagged testing or re-testing, or assigned to QA Testing. Late = pending past deadline.
Waiting for retestIssues in QA Backlog or In QA, by who filed them. Oldest = days waiting.
Retest queue by personTasks and issues in QA Backlog and In QA per assignee or project. Every number opens its tasks.
Issue trendLast 7 days (IST): bugs and feedback filed (created, light) and resolved (pushed to QA Backlog that day, dark). Pick one assignee or project, or see all. Click a point for that day's issues.
Issue ageingOpen issues by days past deadline (same as task ageing). On Hold counts here.
Issues by stageIssues per stage and person (or project). Red = overdue part. Bug / Feedback narrows it.
Issue listsLatest filed, Latest resolved and All issues. Any number above opens All issues narrowed to it. Edit in place, bulk edit and Excel export work as on Development.

Issue lists

Latest filed, Latest resolved and All issues (follows the last number you clicked), with the same editing and export as on Development.

Access permissions

Rights per person or department

Admins

Who sees what and who may change what, set per person or department, with every change logged.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
Access at a glanceHow many rights are set per person and per department, how many admins, and who sees all data. Click the first two to switch the view.
Rights per person or departmentEach column is a right: data (all or own tasks), KPI, Support, Inbox, Employees, edit (all fields or limited) and Admin. Click a value to inherit it, allow it or deny it. The dot shows where it comes from: grey the role, blue the department, teal the person. Locked admins cannot be changed.
Recent changesEvery change: who made it, for whom, and what changed. A change applies from the person’s next click.

The rights

RightValuesWhat it controls
DataAll data / Own tasksEvery project and person, or only tasks assigned to or created by them.
KPIOn / OffThe KPI page.
SupportOn / OffThe Support page and its feedback form.
InboxOn / OffThe Support feedback inbox.
EmployeesOn / OffCompany tree and People directory.
EditAll fields / LimitedEvery task field, or only stage, status and tags.
AdminOn / OffThis page.

Each right is inherited unless set: from the role, then from departments (the deeper one wins), then the person's own setting. A dot shows where it comes from: grey the role, blue a department, teal the person, red a locked admin. Admins listed in the configuration are locked and keep every right.

Definitions

The rules behind the numbers

TermRule
PendingBehind QA Backlog and not on hold: To do, In progress, Pushed to Master, Merged to Development, Pending for QA, Issue Raised.
On holdIn On Hold: neither pending nor completed. Pending + completed + on hold = total.
Completed (done)Completed: in QA Backlog, In QA, Done or LIVE.
OverduePending, not On Hold, and the deadline has passed.
Done todayReached QA Backlog for the first time today (IST).
Due todayDeadline is today (IST), in any stage except LIVE.
ReworkIssues QA sent back after testing: from QA Backlog, In QA or Done to Issue Raised, To do or In progress. Stage flow only.
Retesting awaitedEvery task waiting for QA: in QA Backlog or In QA, split into dev tasks and issues (bug or feedback).
Closed (KPI)Tasks closed in the period (first QA Backlog, else the Bitrix24 closed date). Clean-ups of 30+ tasks closed in one project on one day are left out.
On-time (KPI)Of the tasks closed in the period that had a deadline, the share closed on or before it.
Working timeWorking time: Monday to Friday, 09:30 to 18:30 IST, public holidays excluded.
