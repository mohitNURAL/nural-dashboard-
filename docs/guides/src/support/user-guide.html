01

Welcome

What the dashboard is for

Nural Pulse, the live heartbeat of every project and person, shows work in one place, live from Bitrix24: tasks, deadlines, bugs and feedback, and how things are going. A change made in Bitrix24 reaches the dashboard within seconds, and a change made on the dashboard is made in Bitrix24, in your name.

This guide is for support team. Every number, list, chart and export counts only your own tasks: those assigned to you or created by you. It covers the pages you can open: Development, QA, Support, Daily update, Insights, Workflow, Project types, Feedback, Documents, FAQs.

02

Signing in

Your Bitrix24 account is your key

Open the dashboard and choose Sign in with Bitrix24. Use your Nural Bitrix24 account; the dashboard never sees your password.

  • You stay signed in for 3 days. Then the dashboard shows Your session ended; sign in again from there.
  • Sign out is in your profile menu (your initials, top right).
  • If a change to a task says Bitrix24 refused it, sign out and in again so the dashboard picks up your Bitrix24 permissions.
After an update A toast says a new version is ready and lists what is new. Click Refresh to get it.
03

What you can see and do

Your access

As a member of the Tech Support team you open Development, QA, Support, Daily update, Insights, Workflow, Project types, Feedback, Documents, FAQs. Every number, list, chart and export counts only your own tasks: those assigned to you or created by you. You can change the stage, status and tags of your tasks.

Rights can change. Admins can widen or narrow anyone's access per person or per department; the menu always shows exactly what you can open.
04

Finding your way

Menu, top bar and page tools

The top bar

  • Where you are: e.g. Dashboard › QA.
  • Alerts (bell): overdue tasks and tasks stuck over 5 days in one stage (yours); click for the list.
  • Live: green when updates are flowing in from Bitrix24.
  • Profile: your name and role, the theme (Light, Dark, System), live-update toasts and Sign out.
05

Reading the numbers

Tiles, cards, filters and drill-downs

  • Filters under the top bar (project, assignee, tag or bug/feedback, date range) narrow every number on the page. Reset filters clears them.
  • The i next to any number says exactly what it counts, and what your filters cover.
  • Click any number, bar or cell to see the tasks behind it: the page dims, the number stays lit and its list opens in the middle of the screen. Escape or ✕ puts it back.
  • Switches at the top (Day / Week / Month, All / Dev / Issues, 7 / 14 / 30 days) change only some data points; the guided tour lights up exactly which.
One number, two places. The same data point can show a different number on two pages, because each counts within its own department or group: Development counts the Development department, QA the bugs and feedback, KPI the whole company, Insights the period you chose. Click the number to see exactly which tasks it counts.
Your scope. Every number, list, chart and export counts only your own tasks: those assigned to you or created by you.
06

Insights

What is holding us back

My insights looks at your own tasks: what is overdue, what is due soon, and where your work is split across projects.

  • Choose the period (7, 14 or 30 days) at the top.
  • Every finding names its numbers; click it to list the tasks behind it.
  • Leave and attendance from Infowan join the findings once Infowan is connected; until then Insights uses Bitrix24 and dashboard data only.
07

Daily update

Plan your day, wrap it up, post to Slack

The Daily update icon in the page tools on the right opens it; it never opens by itself. It is also under My work › Daily update, with your last 30 days.

  • Morning: Plan your day. Your overdue and due-today tasks are listed. Untick what you will not do, add any of your other open tasks, write a note and Post my plan. Posting again the same day changes the same Slack message.
  • Evening: Wrap up your day. Your plan comes back with each task Done or WIP. Ticking Done on an open task completes it in Bitrix24, in your name. Work outside the plan becomes a new Bitrix24 task assigned to you (completed at once if you mark it Done). The wrap-up is posted in the same Slack thread.
  • Signing out after posting a plan opens the wrap-up first: Post and sign out or Sign out without posting.
Where it goes. Posts go to the daily work update group on Slack, with your name, once an admin has set the channel (Integration › Connections). Until then your update is saved on the dashboard only, and the window says so.
08

Changing tasks

Edit in place and in bulk

Changes are made in Bitrix24, in your name, and show on the dashboard straight away. You can change the stage, status and tags of your tasks.

  • Edit in place: click a Stage, Status or Tags cell (or a fact in the task view) and pick the new value.
  • Bulk edit: tick rows (or the header box for the whole list), choose what to change, then Check. Rows that cannot take the change turn red with the reason; Apply changes the rest. Up to 100 tasks at a time.
  • Excel: every list downloads as it is filtered and sorted.
Bitrix24 decides. If Bitrix24 refuses a change (for example, no rights on that task), the dashboard says why and nothing changes.
09

Make it yours

Arrange, hide, focus and export

  • Drag by ⠿ to put tiles, cards and lists in your order. Each stays in its own group, and cards resize to fill their row: drop a half-width card next to a full one and both become half.
  • Hide a tile or card with the eye; bring it back with Customise. Task lists cannot be hidden.
  • Tables: Columns chooses and orders the columns (or drag a column header); drag a row by ⠿ to put rows in your own order (Your order). Excel files follow your columns and order.
  • Export page in the page tools: every data point on the page as one Excel workbook or a PDF.
  • Your layout is saved for you and follows you to any computer. Reset layout puts everything back.

Focus view and export

Click a card's title (or an empty part of it) to bring it to the centre, larger, with the page blurred behind. Then Excel exports that card's data, and PDF saves a picture of the whole card with the filters and time on it. Press Escape or click outside to put it back.

Guided tour and what's new

Let the dashboard show you around

  • On your first three sign-ins each page explains itself when you open it: your role first, then every tile, card and list you can see. The last step offers the next page.
  • Any time: the Guided tour icon on the right, or ? on a single data point.
  • Voice: turn it on to hear each step read aloud (browser voice; Edge sounds most natural).
  • What's new: after an update, the refresh toast lists the changes, the page with something new gets a dot, and opening it walks you through.

Help

Common questions

A number looks different from Bitrix24

Check the filters at the top and the switch (All, Dev, Issues); the i says what each number counts. You see only your own tasks. Changes arrive within seconds; if Live is not green, refresh.

I cannot change a task

You can change the stage, status and tags of your tasks. Other fields are for team leads, PM and admins. If Bitrix24 refuses, the message says why. Signing out and in again picks up new Bitrix24 permissions.

I was signed out

Sessions last 3 days from sign-in. Sign in again from the page that opens.

I need a page I do not see

Pages follow your rights. Ask a dashboard admin.

My layout looks odd

Use Reset layout in the page tools on the right.

Still stuck? Ask a dashboard admin, or use the Support page to tell us.
