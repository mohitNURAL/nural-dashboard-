01

On every page

The parts every page shares

PartWhat it does
The menuEvery page is here: the dashboard tabs and, for managers, the Employee module. Hover the icons to open it; click ☰ to pin it open, and again to unpin.
FiltersProject, assignee, tag and date range narrow every number on the tab, within your own tasks. The "i" icons say exactly what each number counts.
AlertsThe bell counts overdue tasks and tasks stuck over 5 days in one stage. Click it for the list and jump to the tasks.
LiveLive means changes in Bitrix24 reach the dashboard within seconds; numbers refresh by themselves. Offline means the connection dropped and it is reconnecting.
Page toolsOn the right: Customise (choose the tiles and cards to show), the guided tour of this page, the PDF guides, reading tours aloud, and resetting the layout. Hover any icon for its name. Each data point also has a small toolbar on hover: ⠿ to move it, ? to explain it, the eye to hide it.
Your profileYour name and role, the theme (Light, Dark, System), live-update toasts, Reset layout, this tour (replay, voice on or off) and Sign out. You are signed out 3 days after signing in.
02

Development

Tasks, issues and deadlines

The Development department's tasks and issues. The All / Dev / Issues switch at the top steers every number.

Every number on this page counts only your own tasks: assigned to you or created by you.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
All, Dev or IssuesDevelopment department only. Dev = development tasks, Issues = bugs and feedback, All = both. Every number on this tab follows this switch.
TotalYour tasks (assigned to you or created by you) for the switch. Click any count to list those tasks below.
PendingBehind QA Backlog and not on hold: To do, In progress, Pushed to Master, Merged to Development, Pending for QA, Issue Raised. Click any count to list those tasks below.
On holdIn On Hold: neither pending nor completed. Pending + completed + on hold = total.
Ready to deployIn the Done stage, ready to go live. Then Deployed: in the LIVE stage.
OverduePending, not On Hold, and the deadline has passed. The first number to act on each day.
Done todayReached QA Backlog for the first time today (IST).
Per person or projectPer person or project. Done = sent to QA (QA Backlog to LIVE). Comp. today = reached QA Backlog today. Switch Assignee / Project at the top. Every count opens its tasks. Click the title to focus it in the centre; drag ⠿ at the top to move it.
Pending vs DonePending vs completed (QA Backlog, In QA, Done, LIVE) per person or project, top 15 by total. Tooltip splits Dev / Issues (Bug / Feedback on Issues). Click a bar to open those tasks.
Task ageingOpen tasks by days past deadline. On Hold counts here but not as Overdue. The darker the red, the later. Chart or Table at the top.
On-time rankingTasks sent to QA with a deadline. Ranked by on-time %, then average days early. Fewer than 3: not ranked.
Tasks by stageTasks per Kanban stage; the colour bar shows each stage total. The red part of a cell is overdue. Click a cell for those tasks.
Task listsDue today, Overdue, All tasks and Alert details open here. Clicking any number above opens All tasks narrowed to it. In a list you can change stage, status and tags of your tasks, tick rows for bulk edit, and export to Excel.

Task lists

Due today, Overdue, All tasks (follows the last number you clicked) and Alert details. Each list sorts, edits in place, bulk edits, pages and exports to Excel.

03

QA

Bugs, feedback, testing and retesting

Bugs and feedback (tasks tagged bug or feedback), testing work and the retest queue. The Bug or Feedback filter narrows the page.

Every number on this page counts only your own tasks: assigned to you or created by you.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
Issues filedBugs and feedback assigned to you or filed by you. Click any count to list those tasks below.
Open / pendingOpen = pending: any stage behind QA Backlog except On Hold (Issue Raised included). Resolved = QA Backlog, In QA, Done or LIVE. On Hold is its own tile. The ring shows the resolved %.
On holdIn On Hold: neither pending nor completed. Pending + completed + on hold = total.
Last filed and resolvedLatest day an issue was created, and how many that day. Next to it, Last resolved: Latest day issues were pushed to QA Backlog (a fix handed to QA), and how many that day.
ReworkIssues QA sent back after testing: from QA Backlog, In QA or Done to Issue Raised, To do or In progress. Stage flow only.
Retesting awaitedEvery task waiting for QA: in QA Backlog or In QA, split into dev tasks and issues (bug or feedback). The QA team's queue.
Issues by personOpen vs resolved, each split into bug and feedback (or the reverse), per person or project. Table: the assignee-wise table. Click the title to focus it in the centre; drag ⠿ at the top to move it.
Rework by developerQA send-backs per person or project, split into bug and feedback, and the % of their issues sent back. First Issue Raised to To do is not counted.
Testing tasksTasks tagged testing or re-testing, or assigned to QA Testing. Late = pending past deadline.
Waiting for retestIssues in QA Backlog or In QA, by who filed them. Oldest = days waiting.
Retest queue by personTasks and issues in QA Backlog and In QA per assignee or project. Every number opens its tasks.
Issue trendLast 7 days (IST): bugs and feedback filed (created, light) and resolved (pushed to QA Backlog that day, dark). Pick one assignee or project, or see all. Click a point for that day's issues.
Issue ageingOpen issues by days past deadline (same as task ageing). On Hold counts here.
Issues by stageIssues per stage and person (or project). Red = overdue part. Bug / Feedback narrows it.
Issue listsLatest filed, Latest resolved and All issues. Any number above opens All issues narrowed to it. Edit in place, bulk edit and Excel export work as on Development.

Issue lists

Latest filed, Latest resolved and All issues (follows the last number you clicked), with the same editing and export as on Development.

Support

Feedback for the coming Support tab

Coming soon. Until then it collects what the support team and management need; admins and the Support lead read the answers.

Every data point

Data pointWhat it shows and how to use it
The Support tab is comingIt will be built from what the support team and management need, so it starts by asking you.
Share your needsTick what you would like to see, how often you would use it, and describe your biggest problem today. Must have, should have or nice to have helps us plan.
My feedbackWhat you sent, and whether it has been reviewed. Only you, the admins and the Support lead see your entries.
Feedback inboxEvery entry from everyone. Filter New or Must have, open one to read it all, add a private note, mark it reviewed, or download all as Excel.
